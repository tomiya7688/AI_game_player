# Coordinated development

Adapt the four roles to the task; one agent can hold multiple roles. Delegate only when authorized and beneficial. Assign nonoverlapping ownership; the lead integrates and verifies the final changes. Reviewers are read-only and do not delegate. Follow current project review procedures, free-model restrictions, evidence rules and permissions. Do not request Haiku or Copilot reviews under the current policy. Measure before optimization; preserve behavior and tests.
